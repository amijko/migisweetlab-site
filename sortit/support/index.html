FAQ

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?
Progress is stored on your device. It moves to a new phone only through your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup).

Does the game need the internet?
No, all levels are playable offline.

How do I turn off analytics?
Settings → Analytics. See the Privacy Policy for details.
