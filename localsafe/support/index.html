FAQ

I forgot my master password. Can you recover it?
No. The master password is never stored, and the vault can't be opened without it — not by us, not by anyone. Keep it somewhere safe, for example written down at home.

How do I turn on autofill on iPhone?
Open Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords and turn on Local Safe (on iOS 17: Settings → Passwords → Password Options).

How do I turn on autofill on Android and in Chrome?
Open Local Safe Settings → Autofill and choose it as the autofill service (in Android settings this is usually Passwords, passkeys & accounts → Preferred service). Then in Chrome open ⋮ → Settings → Autofill services, choose Autofill using another service and restart Chrome.

Can I use my vault on a computer?
Yes — open the same file in KeePassXC and use the KeePassXC-Browser extension in Chrome. See the steps.

How do I sync between my phone and other devices?
Keep the vault file in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another synced folder and open it from there. The cloud app syncs the file; Local Safe re-reads it and merges changes before every save.

How do I back up my vault?
Your vault is one .kdbx file. Copy it somewhere safe from time to time — a computer, a USB stick or a second cloud. Cloud drives also keep older versions of the file.

I used Safe+ (mySafebox). How do I move my data?
Open Settings → Import → Safe+. On Android choose the folder mySafeboxPro/DB in the phone storage. On iPhone first copy the DB folder to the Files app, then choose it.

Does the app need the internet?
No. It has no network code, no analytics and no ads. Syncing is done by your cloud app, not by Local Safe.

Is it free?
Yes. No ads and no subscription.
