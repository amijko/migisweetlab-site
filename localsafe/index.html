From the notebook to the vault

The notebook by the router has had a good run.

On paper or in Notes

  • Anyone who finds it can read it
  • Lost with the bag, the phone case or the drawer
  • You still type every password by hand
  • Short, reused passwords because long ones don’t fit

In Local Safe

  • Encrypted with AES-256 — unreadable without your master password
  • A backup copy is one file you can keep anywhere
  • Logins fill in apps and websites by themselves
  • A generator makes a long, unique password for every site

Already saved them in Chrome or another app? Import them in a minute: Chrome, Apple Passwords, Firefox, Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass, KeePassXC and Safe+.

What it never does

Trust you can check before you install.

Look at the permissions on Google Play or the privacy label on the App Store: there is nothing to see.

  • Connects to the internet
  • Asks you to make an account
  • Stores your master password
  • Shows ads or tracks you
  • Locks your data into its own format

Phone, cloud, computer

1On your phoneCreate a vault, unlock with Face ID or a fingerprint, autofill in apps and browsers.
2In your cloudKeep the .kdbx file in iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Local Safe merges changes before it saves.
3On your computerOpen the same file in KeePassXC; KeePassXC-Browser fills logins in Chrome.

On the computer: in KeePassXC open Tools → Settings → Browser Integration and tick Chrome, then press Connect in the extension. KeePassXC is a free open-source project, not affiliated with Migisweetlab.
