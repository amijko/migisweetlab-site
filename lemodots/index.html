Wear OS

The same dots on your wrist.

A watch face with the Nexus 4 dots slowly shimmering behind the time, plus weather, date, battery, steps and heart rate. In always-on mode it switches to a dim, battery-friendly version.

Quiet by design

No ads. No tracking. Not even internet access.

The phone app has no internet permission, no accounts and no analytics; rendering runs on the GPU and pauses as soon as the wallpaper is hidden. Privacy policy.
