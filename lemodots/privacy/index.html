Lemo Dots — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the Lemo Dots live wallpaper for Android phones and tablets and the Lemo Dots watch face for Wear OS (together, "the app"), published on Google Play by Migisweetlab.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the phone app does not even have permission to access the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and saved themes (colors, dot size, shape, animation options) are stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, the system may include them in your device backup; that backup is handled by Google under your account settings, and we have no access to it.
  • Motion sensors. If you turn on tilt lighting or tilt parallax, the wallpaper reads the gyroscope while it is visible to move the light and the grid. These readings are used instantly for drawing and are never saved or sent anywhere.

The Wear OS watch face

The watch face shows the time, date, weather, battery level, step count and heart rate. All of these values are supplied by Wear OS on your watch and are only drawn on the screen. The watch face contains no code of its own, does not store these values and cannot send them anywhere. Wear OS may ask you for permission before showing your heart rate; you can refuse or change this at any time in the watch settings.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
